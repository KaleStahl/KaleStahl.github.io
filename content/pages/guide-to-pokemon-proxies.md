# What are Proxies?

Proxy cards are unofficial cards which stand in place (proxy) for official cards. These could be as simple as a basic energy card you write the name of a card on, or high quality imitation cards that are nearly indistinguishable from the official product. Now, the legality of proxies is not super defined, but be advised that none of this article is legal advice, and I do not condone selling proxies.
