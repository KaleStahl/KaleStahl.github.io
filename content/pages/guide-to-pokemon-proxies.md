# What are Proxies?

Proxy cards are unofficial cards which stand in place (proxy) for official cards. These could be as simple as a basic energy card you write the name of a card on, or high quality imitation cards that are nearly indistinguishable from the official product. Now, the legality of proxies is not super defined, so be advised that none of this article is legal advice.

---

# How to make Proxies
There are three main ways to make proxies:
- 1: Use a random placeholder card/backwards card in place of the desired card.
- 2: Print a low quality image, print it out on plain paper and stick it in front of a basic energy card in a sleeve.
- 3: Print a high quality image on high quality cardstock to imitate real cards as closely as possible.

Type 1 requires almost no work (other than remembering which card it's supposed to be), but also looks and feels bad to play.
